INV-103 appears as PAY-003 and PAY-005, both for ₹12,000 and the same vendor. The register totals ₹64,500; retaining one payment per invoice gives ₹52,500. The difference is ₹12,000.

This is a suspected duplicate, not proof of fraud. Check the invoice, bank references, reversals and any authorised instalments before deciding whether recovery is needed.
